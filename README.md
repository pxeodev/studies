## Crypto AI Benchmark v2

Six AI tools answered the same nine trader questions in March 2026. Each of the 54 answers was
scored on five weighted dimensions: comprehension 20%, actionability 25%, originality 15%,
accuracy 25% and risk awareness 15%. Shumi was left out of the scored field.

- `benchmark/data.js` holds all 270 scores.
- `benchmark/index.html` renders the rankings, the per-question breakdown and the heatmap.
- One AI evaluator scored every answer, and answers were collected over about 26 hours.
